# OVERLANE — Project Vision

## Concept

OVERLANE is an original arcade highway-racing game for PC. The player drives a fictional high-performance vehicle through dense moving traffic, using speed, spatial reading, and precise lane choices to win short point-to-point races.

## Audience and product goals

The game targets players who enjoy immediately readable, controller-friendly arcade driving and repeatable competitive sessions. A match should take roughly three to six minutes, restart quickly, and reward both safe momentum and bold, skilled passes.

The initial commercial MVP is one fictional highway route, one playable vehicle, Traffic Sprint, solo play, and small private online sessions. The first vertical slice is local solo only.

## Design pillars

1. **Sense of speed:** strong visual, camera, and audio speed cues while retaining readability.
2. **Dense but fair traffic:** difficult traffic patterns with configurable reaction-time and time-to-collision safeguards.
3. **Responsive arcade handling:** stable, weighted, speed-sensitive controls for keyboard and controller.
4. **Competitive traffic interaction:** position, drafting, lane pressure, and traffic reading—not weapons—create rivalry.
5. **Short sessions:** minimal downtime from menu to rematch.

## Boundaries

No licensed brands, ripped assets, open world, pedestrians, battle pass, paid microtransactions, dedicated servers, or host migration are in the initial release scope. All branding, vehicles, roads, and audio must be original or licensed with recorded proof.
